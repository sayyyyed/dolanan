Physics formulas
Motion update: v(t+Δt) = v(t) + a·Δt
Position update: x(t+Δt) = x(t) + v(t+Δt)·Δt
Gravity: a = (0, -9.81, 0)
Step size: Δt = 0.016s
Bounce: v' = -0.78·v when a body hits the floor or walls.
